GSTR-1 vs GSTR-3B: what is the difference?

By Ravi Teja, SLV Online Service Center, Yadiki. Updated 19 October 2026.

Regular GST taxpayers file two main returns. They look similar but do different jobs.

GSTR-1: details of sales

It lists your outward supplies, invoice by invoice. It helps your customers claim input tax credit. The usual monthly due date is the 11th of the next month.

GSTR-3B: summary and payment

It is a summary of sales, input tax credit, and the tax you pay. The usual monthly due date is the 20th of the next month.

Quarterly option

Small taxpayers under the QRMP scheme can file quarterly and pay tax monthly. Dates differ, so confirm yours.

Tip

Make sure GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B figures match. A mismatch is a common reason for notices.

Due dates can be extended by the government, so check the latest notification.
