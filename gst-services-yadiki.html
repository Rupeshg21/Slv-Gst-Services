GST Registration and Return Filing in Yadiki, Anantapur

Since 2016 we have helped shops, traders, and service providers in Yadiki and Anantapur district stay compliant with GST, from first registration to every monthly return.

What we do

  • New GST registration and GSTIN follow-up
  • Monthly and quarterly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filing
  • Nil return filing
  • GST amendments and cancellation
  • Replies to GST notices

Documents to bring

  • PAN and Aadhaar of the owner
  • Photo and business address proof
  • Bank account proof
  • Sales and purchase invoices for return filing

Frequently asked questions

Who needs GST registration?

Usually businesses above the turnover limit for goods or services, and some businesses regardless of turnover. Ask us to check your case.

How long does registration take?

If documents are clear, a GSTIN usually arrives within about a week.

Do I file a return if I had no sales?

Yes. A nil return is still required to avoid late fees.
