Income Tax Return (ITR) Filing in Yadiki, Anantapur

We prepare and file income tax returns for salaried people, small business owners, and pensioners, and check each return against AIS and Form 26AS before submitting.

What we do

  • ITR for salaried income and pension
  • ITR for business and professionals
  • Refund claims for TDS
  • Capital gains reporting
  • E-verification support

Documents to bring

  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Form 16, if salaried
  • AIS and Form 26AS
  • Bank statements and interest certificates
  • Investment and insurance proofs

Frequently asked questions

What is the usual ITR due date?

For individuals who need no audit it is usually 31 July, unless extended.

What if I miss the date?

A late fee and interest can apply, so file as early as you can.

Do I need to file if my tax is zero?

Sometimes yes, for example to claim a refund or when specific conditions apply.
