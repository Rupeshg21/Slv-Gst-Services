Tally vs Excel for small business accounting

By Ravi Teja, SLV Online Service Center, Yadiki. Updated 19 October 2026.

Both tools can keep a small business organised. The right choice depends on size and how many transactions you have.

Excel

  • Good for very small shops with few daily entries
  • Flexible and familiar
  • Needs discipline, because mistakes are easy and nothing is locked

Tally

  • Built for accounting, with ledgers, vouchers, and balance sheet
  • Prepares GST reports and helps with returns
  • Better for many daily transactions and stock

Our advice

If you issue more than a few bills a day or deal in stock, move to accounting software. If you are just starting, keep clean Excel records and upgrade as you grow. We can set up either for you.
