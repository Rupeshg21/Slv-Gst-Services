Bookkeeping basics for small businesses in India

By Ravi Teja, SLV Online Service Center, Yadiki. Updated 19 October 2026.

Bookkeeping means recording every sale, purchase, and expense in an organised way. For a small shop or service business, it is the base for GST returns, income tax, and loan applications.

Records to keep

  • Sales invoices and daily cash sales
  • Purchase bills from suppliers
  • Expense vouchers such as rent, salary, and electricity
  • Bank statements and cash book
  • Stock register, if you trade in goods

Good habits

  • Record entries weekly, not at year end
  • Keep every bill, paper or digital
  • Match your books with the bank statement monthly
  • Keep personal and business money separate

Why it matters

Accurate books let you claim input tax credit correctly, avoid GST notices, and show a clear profit to banks. We keep GST-ready books for businesses in Yadiki and Anantapur.
