7 common GST filing mistakes small businesses make

By Ravi Teja, SLV Online Service Center, Yadiki. Updated 19 October 2026.

Most GST notices come from a few repeated errors. Check yourself against this list.

  1. Filing late, which brings late fees and interest on tax due
  2. Skipping nil returns when there were no sales
  3. Claiming input tax credit without the supplier showing the invoice
  4. Mismatch between GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B
  5. Wrong HSN or tax rate on invoices
  6. Mixing personal and business expenses in input tax credit
  7. Not reconciling purchases with GSTR-2B

How to avoid them

Keep books updated monthly, reconcile purchases before filing, and calendar the due dates. Our accounting service does this every month for small businesses in Yadiki.
